Hi there! I'm an applied development micro-economist focused on poverty and inequality in lower-income countries, primarily in Africa. Specifically, my work centres on labour markets and policy evaluation, with a focus on social protection, education, and technological change. My research has been published in *World Development*, *Energy Economics*, and *BMC Public Health* among other outlets, and I hold a Y1 rating from South Africa's [National Research Foundation (NRF)](https://www.nrf.ac.za/rating/) awarded through independent international peer review.

I'm currently a Senior Research Officer at the [Development Policy Research Unit (DPRU)](https://commerce.uct.ac.za/dpru) based in the School of Economics at the [University of Cape Town (UCT)](https://uct.ac.za), South Africa; a Consultant for the [World Bank's Social Protection & Jobs Global Practice](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/unit/unit-dec/impactevaluation/programs/SocialProtectionJobs); and a Research Associate at the [Research on Socio-Economic Policy (RESEP) unit](https://resep.sun.ac.za) at Stellenbosch University, South Africa. In 2025, I was a Visiting Scholar at the [United Nations University World Institute for Development Economics Research (UNU-WIDER)](https://www.wider.unu.edu) in Helsinki, Finland in 2025. I regularly do economic advisory work for multilateral organisations such as the World Bank and UNU-WIDER, as well as several departments of the South African government including the Presidency, National Treasury, the Department of Employment and Labour, and the Department of Higher Education and Training.

I earned my PhD from UCT in 2024. Under the supervision of [Haroon Bhorat](https://commerce.uct.ac.za/dpru/prof-haroon-bhorat-profile), my [thesis](http://hdl.handle.net/11427/40999) considers the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on labour market inequality. I earned my undergraduate, honour's, and master's degrees from [Stellenbosch University](http://www.sun.ac.za/english), with [Ingrid Woolard](https://www.saldru.uct.ac.za/staff/ingrid-woolard/) and [Debra Shepherd](https://resep.sun.ac.za/team-member/debra-shepherd/) serving as my co-supervisors for the latter.

On this site, you will find a repository of my work, including publications, working papers, reports, and several resources for research economists I find useful!
